Unified live view

The default field combines timestamped public data with explicit visual mappings. NOAA spacecraft observations supply the solar-wind and magnetic histories. DC is the mean wind speed; AC RMS is the root-mean-square fluctuation around that mean. The Fourier peak uses consecutive one-minute samples and is labeled in millihertz (mHz). Bayer dots encode the received history, the population model drives the signed-distance contour, IJburg model temperature sets the glow, and a display-phase sweep marks time. The sweep is not a radar measurement.

Automatic refresh: NOAA every 5 minutes, IJburg weather every 15 minutes, and Census every 6 hours when enabled. Observation timestamps remain attached to the readings; missing data is not replaced with simulated observations.

02 / Signed distance & relative strain

φ(n,t) = n − P(t)
n = P₀ + probe offset
relative strain = (P(t) − P₀) / P₀ × 10⁶ ppm

The gauge is a one-dimensional signed distance in people, centered on the estimated total P(t). Tare locks a reference P₀, so an unchanged probe drifts negative as population grows. Strain is a mathematical relative change, not measured physical strain. The signed field is not a geographic or three-dimensional surface.

x = 2 × relative strain
Vout / Vex = −x / (4 + 2x) · Vex = 5 V

A virtual quarter bridge maps that relative change to an illustrative voltage, with gauge factor 2 and an R4-arm sign convention. No strain sensor is connected. Tare is a local reference only; changing the source can shift the gauge.

03 / Oscilloscope & spectrum

A seeded spectrum is inverse-transformed into illustrative population deviations. One 16-second cycle repeats. The multimeter computes the mean, AC RMS, and peak-to-peak of the four-second sample window. DC coupling includes φ; AC coupling subtracts the sample mean. The real 512-point FFT uses a Hann window with coherent-gain correction at 128 samples per second. No demographic variability, EEG, or electrical signal is measured.

04 / Virtual FMCW radar

delay = 2R/c · S = bandwidth / chirp time
Doppler = 2v/λ
beat = S × delay − Doppler

A single ideal target with no noise or multipath. Positive velocity approaches. Tx and Rx show normalized instantaneous chirp frequency; IF is a computed beat waveform. Range and speed are independent of world population. A single up-chirp has range–Doppler coupling; the beat alone cannot independently determine both. The simulation uses no transmitting or receiving hardware.

05 / Earth & space

Solar-wind and magnetic readings come from NOAA SWPC upstream spacecraft feeds. Observation timestamps, freshness, and data alignment stay visible in the Earth / Space panel. An 8 × 8 Bayer matrix turns each received value into an ordered dot density. The scanning line is decorative; the dots change only when observations update. These readings do not imply a relationship with population.

06 / Joules, watts & amperes

W(t) = P(t) × w/person
I(t) = W(t) / V
E = ∫ W(t) dt

Session joules integrate the model only while the current timer is running, capped at its deadline. Pauses add nothing. The initial 100 W/person and 230 V are editable illustrative assumptions, not measured physiology, electricity consumption, or harvestable human energy.
